নামভৰ্তি

Join the Niketan family — from Ankur (KG) to Class 10.

How to Apply

A simple, personal process

Admissions are handled directly at the school office. Parents and guardians are welcome to visit the campus near the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple, Bhuragaon.

Steps

  • Visit the school office during working hours
  • Carry the child's birth certificate and previous report card (if applicable)
  • Meet our teachers to understand the Niketan's values and daily routine

Why families choose us

  • Value-based education in the mother tongue
  • Small, caring rural campus in central Bhuragaon
  • Experienced acharyas — 22 teachers for about 565 students
  • Part of the 550+ strong Shishu Shiksha Samiti, Assam family

Come, see the campus

The best way to know the Niketan is to walk through it. Find us beside the Shiva temple in the heart of Bhuragaon.
