Academics

শৈক্ষিক

Twelve classes from Ankur to Class 10, in the Assamese medium.

Curriculum

Learning with values

The Niketan follows the Shishu Shiksha Samiti curriculum with the SEBA pattern at the secondary level — strong academics woven together with Indian culture and character building.

What we teach

  • Core subjects as per the Samiti / SEBA-based syllabus
  • Moral education — stories of heroes, honesty, personal hygiene
  • Physical education & yoga
  • Music and Sanskrit
  • Vedic mathematics

Beyond the classroom

  • School magazine প্ৰান্তিক (Prantik) and the Vidya Bharati Sangbad newsletter
  • Cultural programmes on Indian festivals and traditions
  • Morning prayer with shlokas and patriotic songs
Uniform

Dress code

Boys

White shirt with navy-blue shorts or long pants and black shoes. On Thursdays and Saturdays: all-white uniform with white shoes. A red sweater is permitted in winter.

Girls

Navy-blue skirt or salwar-kameez with white dupatta and black shoes. On Thursdays and Sundays: all-white salwar-kameez with dupatta and white shoes. A red sweater is permitted in winter.

The school badge, ID card and name-belt are part of the uniform — keeping simplicity and discipline at the heart of everyday school life.
