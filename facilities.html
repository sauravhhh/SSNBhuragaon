Campus

What the campus offers

On campus

  • Library with 200–500 books
  • Playground for sports and morning assembly
  • Electricity and hand-pump drinking water
  • Separate toilets for boys and girls
  • Barbed-wire fenced, secure boundary

A central location

The Niketan sits in the heart of Bhuragaon, near the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple — bounded by Bhuragaon College, the police station and the supplementary health centre. Children from every corner of the town reach the campus easily.
