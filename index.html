About Us

Rooted in values, growing with Bhuragaon

A private, co-educational Assamese-medium school run by Shishu Shiksha Samiti, Assam — the state affiliate of Vidya Bharati — nurturing children of the greater Bhuragaon area since 2004.

Shankardev Shishu Niketan, Bhuragaon was established on 31 January 2004 through the efforts of a few dedicated local residents who wanted value-based education for the children of the region. It began humbly with just three classes — Ankur, Mukul and Class 1 — in an open space near the Bhuragaon Hindi School.

The journey was not easy: the original site was destroyed in a fire, and the Niketan had to shift twice before finally finding its permanent home near the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple in the heart of Bhuragaon — bounded by Bhuragaon College to the north, the police station to the east, the Shiva temple to the west and the supplementary health centre to the south.

“স্বয়মেৱ মৃগেন্দ্ৰতা” — be strong through one's own qualities and deeds.

Our guiding philosophy

The Niketan follows the educational vision of Vidya Bharati Akhil Bharatiya Shiksha Sansthan — “Sa Vidya Ya Vimuktaye” (that is knowledge which liberates). Like all schools of Shishu Shiksha Samiti, Assam, it is named in tribute to Mahapurush Srimanta Sankardev, and blends modern academics with Indian culture, moral education and character building.

  • Morning assembly with shlokas and patriotic songs
  • Students of all religions study together
  • Emphasis on discipline, simplicity and service
Academics

From Ankur to Class 10

Twelve classes — Ankur and Mukul (kindergarten) through Class 10 — taught in the Assamese medium, following the Shishu Shiksha Samiti curriculum with the SEBA pattern at the secondary level.

Curriculum

  • Core subjects as per the Samiti / SEBA-based syllabus
  • Moral education — stories of heroes, honesty, personal hygiene
  • Physical education & yoga
  • Music and Sanskrit
  • Vedic mathematics

Beyond the classroom

  • School magazine প্ৰান্তিক (Prantik) and the Vidya Bharati Sangbad newsletter
  • Cultural programmes on Indian festivals and traditions
  • Prescribed uniform with badge, ID card and name-belt — white shirt with navy-blue shorts/trousers for boys and navy-blue skirt or salwar-kameez with white dupatta for girls (all-white on Thursdays & weekends); red sweater permitted in winter
Admissions

Join the Niketan family

Admissions are handled directly at the school office. Parents and guardians are welcome to visit the campus near the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple, Bhuragaon, to enquire about admission to classes from Ankur (KG) to Class 10.

How to apply

  • Visit the school office during working hours
  • Carry the child's birth certificate and previous report card (if applicable)
  • Meet our teachers to understand the Niketan's values and routine

Why families choose us

  • Value-based education in the mother tongue
  • Small, caring rural campus in central Bhuragaon
  • Experienced acharyas — 22 teachers for about 565 students
  • Part of the 550+ strong Shishu Shiksha Samiti, Assam family
