Welcome

Rooted in values, growing with Bhuragaon

A private, co-educational Assamese-medium school run by Shishu Shiksha Samiti, Assam — the state affiliate of Vidya Bharati — nurturing the children of greater Bhuragaon for over two decades.

About Us

Founded on 31 January 2004 by dedicated local residents, the Niketan began with three classes in an open field — and rose from a devastating fire to its permanent campus by the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple.

Our story →

Academics

Twelve classes from Ankur to Class 10 in the Assamese medium — SEBA-based learning enriched with moral education, yoga, music, Sanskrit and Vedic mathematics.

Admissions at the school office

Parents and guardians are welcome to visit the campus near the Barduba Tup Shiva Temple to enquire about admission from Ankur (KG) to Class 10.
